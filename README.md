# SHOW UP

### One campus. One feed. Just show up.

**Show Up** is a campus activity platform built for students to discover, host, join, and actually participate in activities happening around them.

From sports and study sessions to photo walks and social activities, Show Up puts everything in one place instead of scattering activities across group chats and different platforms.

## ✨ What Can You Do With Show Up?

### 🏃 Discover Activities

Browse activities happening around campus and find something you want to join.

### 📌 Host Activities

Students can create their own activities with details such as:

* Activity name
* Category
* Date & time
* Venue
* Capacity
* Activity details

### 🤝 Join & Leave

Students can join activities they are interested in and leave whenever they want.

### 👥 See Who's Coming

Activity pages show the people who have joined, making it easier to know who you'll be participating with.

### ✅ Activity Check-In

Participants can check in shortly before an activity starts.

The check-in window opens **10 minutes before the activity** and closes **10 minutes after it starts**.

This creates a simple attendance system based on actually showing up.

### 📊 Show-Up Record

Your participation matters.

Show Up keeps track of whether participants actually attend the activities they joined, creating a **Show-Up Record** that represents their participation history.

### 🎟️ Activity Stubs

After completing an activity, participants receive a unique **Show-Up Stub**.

The stub acts as a shareable record of participation that can also be used for social-media promotion.

### 👤 Profiles

Users can create and edit their profiles and see information about themselves and their activities.

### 🔐 Authentication

Users can securely:

* Sign up
* Log in
* Log out
* Manage their account

## 💡 The Problem

Campus activities are often scattered across:

* WhatsApp groups
* Instagram
* Club groups
* Friend circles
* Word of mouth

This makes it difficult for students to discover activities outside their existing social circles.

**Show Up brings those activities together into one campus-focused platform.**
